// hud.overlay.v1 + scramjet

A heads-up glass
dock for any page.

A floating HUD of frosted, glowing panels — featuring the Scramjet proxy browser, test canvas widget, performance monitors, chat, and Glass Lab controls.

⬡ Liquid Glass HUD LIVE Drag, don't click — drag this to your bookmarks bar to install it.
1. Show your bookmarks bar (Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + B).
2. Drag the ⬡ Liquid Glass HUD button up onto the bar.
3. Visit any site, click the bookmark. Click it again to hide the HUD.
